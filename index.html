About ECOAST

A Academy Built for the Future

Since 2001, the Engineering Computing Academy of Science and Technology has blended rigorous academics with real-world practice.

Our Mission

To empower the next generation of engineers, computer scientists and technologists with the knowledge, ethics and hands-on skills to solve the world's toughest challenges — from sustainable energy to artificial intelligence.

Why Students Choose ECOAST

  • Industry internships — guaranteed placement support with 300+ partners

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the entry requirements?

Diploma programs require a secondary-school certificate; bachelor's programs require passes in Mathematics, English and two science subjects (or equivalent). Master's applicants need a recognised bachelor's degree. Mature and transfer students are welcome.

Does ECOAST offer online or evening classes?

Yes. Most computing and technology programs offer evening, weekend and blended online options so working students can study flexibly.

Is accommodation available on campus?

Yes — our hostels house 2,000+ students with Wi-Fi, security, study rooms and meal plans. Hostel places are allocated first-come, first-served.

How do scholarships work?

Every applicant is automatically considered. Merit awards are based on entrance results, while need-based and Women-in-STEM grants require a short additional statement in your application.

When do classes start?

The 2026/2027 academic year begins in January 2027 for most programs, with a September 2026 early-start option for selected diplomas and certificates.
